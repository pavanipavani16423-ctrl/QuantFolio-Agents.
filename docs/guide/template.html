What it is

A money manager's routine, done by a team of AI helpers.

A family or charity has savings in company shares and rules like "exactly 8 companies", "at most 20% in one", "no oil and gas". Every few months someone must read reports, choose what to buy and sell, check each rule and explain it. This project does that, shows every reason, and lets a person decide.

Advisors and portfolio managers

Prepare a rebalancing plan for each client in a minute, with a plain-language memo they can send.

Compliance teams

Every client rule is read from the client's own document and checked, with a link to where it is written.

Risk and IT teams

Personal data is removed, hidden instructions are stopped, trades need a signed approval, and every decision is in a tamper-evident log.

The team

Agents, tools, and how they talk.

A supervisor decides who works next. Two agents run as separate services, as if they belonged to other teams, and receive tasks over A2A (agent-to-agent). Tools are shared through MCP, a standard toolbox where each agent may only use what its job needs.

Hybrid search (RAG)

It looks things up before it answers.

The research agent only answers from passages it found in real reports, and cites them. It searches two ways at once, merges the results, and a small AI "reranker" puts the best passages first.

Safety, simply

Checked at every door.

Plain rules, not AI, check every place where text comes in or goes out, so they cannot be talked out of a decision. Click an example to see what the real safety check says (these verdicts were produced by running the project's own code).

Your request

Personal data (PAN, Aadhaar, phone, email, card) is removed. Hidden instructions are found by patterns and by meaning. Insider trading, manipulation and promises of returns are refused.

Documents read

A report passage that tries to give the AI orders is set aside. Sources are marked as data, never instructions.

The answer

Every source number must exist and really support its sentence. Numbers must match the facts; no stock outside the plan; no promises. A failed check gets one rewrite, then a safe fallback.

Memory

It remembers what you taught it.

Like a good assistant, the team keeps four kinds of memory. It writes memory only from your decisions, checks it before reusing it, and you can make it forget a client at any time.

Working

Everything in the current run, saved after each step, so a plan waiting for you survives a restart.

Past reviews

"Last time Priya approved an 8-stock plan." Used to explain what changed since then.

Preferences

"Avoid ONGC", "at most 15% per stock", "lower risk". They make the client's rules stricter, never looser.

How to write

Your notes, like "keep it simple for the trustees", reused as guidance for the memo. Notes that look like orders to the AI are not kept.

A research memory also reuses a checked answer when the same question about the same companies comes back, saving time and cost.

Human in the loop

You decide. It learns.

Nothing is traded until a person approves. If you change something, the agents re-plan at once, and (if you tick "remember") follow your preference for this client from then on.
