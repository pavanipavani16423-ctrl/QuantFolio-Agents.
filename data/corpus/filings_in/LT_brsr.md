7. Statement by dir ector responsible for the business responsibility report, highlighting ESG related challenges, targets and achievements.
L&T’s commitment to the society and the environment, embedded in our Sustainability Vision ‘For a Better World’ , continues to be a fundamental driver of long-term value creation and business resilience. In an increasingly volatile global environment marked by geopolitical uncertainties and supply chain disruptions, commitments to environmental and social priorities are being tested worldwide. However, in alignment with national priorities and our ethos, sustainability remains strategic imperatives for the Company.
As a part of our commitment to envir onmental stewardship, the Company has set ambitious targets of achieving Carbon Neutrality by 2040 and Water Neutrality by 2035. These goals have been operationalised through structured five-year cycles aligned with business strategy planning under Lakshya. During the first cycle (FY 2020-21 to FY 2025-26), we achieved a 32% reduction in GHG emissions intensity, primarily driven by a corresponding reduction in energy intensity.
While further decarbonisation opportunities r emain, particularly through increased adoption of renewable electricity, currently at 19%, the Company is adopting Group Captive renewable power arrangements for key manufacturing facilities and offices. Our businesses continue to focus on targeted initiatives including energy efficiency improvements, adoption of low- and zero-carbon fuels and scaling renewable energy usage.
Progress towards Water Neutrality is being driven through a multi-pronged approach encompassing water use efficiency, wastewater recycling and increased reliance on non-freshwater sources. Rainwater harvesting continues to be a key lever, with enhanced capacity created at major manufacturing locations. Across EPC project sites, wastewater recycling reached 2.7 million kl in FY 2025-26.
In addition, water stewar dship efforts are complemented by community-based interventions under our CSR programmes.
Independent assessments of water conservation potential across project locations indicate that approximately 4 million kl water were conserved during the year. These efforts contributed to a 25% reduction in net freshwater withdrawal intensity compared to the previous year, reinforcing our path towards Water Neutrality by 2035.
Recognising sustainability as a key business opportunity, L&T developed a portfolio of ‘Gr een Business’ offerings that enable customers to reduce environmental impact and optimise resource efficiency. This portfolio continues to expand, contributing meaningfully to revenue and positioning the Company to capitalise on emerging low-carbon and sustainable infrastructure opportunities. For FY 2025-26, the ‘Green Business’ portfolio revenue stood at ~R 78,700 Cr., contributing 51% to the Company’s total revenue. L&T’s leadership in offering solutions for a sustainable future has also been recognized by Engineering News-Record (ENR), one of the globally recognised publications in the construction industry and we have enhanced our standing to 2 nd from 3rd rank in Top 200 Environmental Firms Survey conducted annually.
On the social fr ont, Health and safety remain paramount. A strengthened Corporate EHS function drives implementation of our safety strategy, ‘VISION KNOW HARM’ , focused on proactive risk identification and strict adherence to safety protocols. During FY 2025-26, we have recorded 7.4 million safety training hours and over 3,75,000 contract workers underwent structured health and safety training, reinforcing a strong safety culture across operations.
Corporate Social Responsibility has long been integral to L&T’ s values. In FY 2025-26, CSR initiatives positively impacted approximately 1.9 million lives across India. Our flagship programme, Unnati (Integrated Community Development Programme), continues to enhance resilience in water-stressed regions, benefiting over 30,000 households and significantly improving groundwater levels. New initiatives such as Nayi Disha focus on strengthening communities of migrant construction workers, while Project Jyoti is transforming STEM education in government schools through digital infrastructure and experiential learning, improving student outcomes and engagement. The Company is actively scaling these proven models to maximise impact across geographies, contributing to inclusive growth, reduced inequalities and community empowerment.
Beyond programmes, we are strengthening systems, processes and disclosures. We remain committed to aligning with global standards, enhancing transparency and integrating sustainability across the value chain, from employee well-being and circular economy practices to clean energy transition and responsible supply chains.
‘Every great story begins with a leap’ reflects our belief in taking purposeful strides - leveraging technology, raising our ambitions and delivering measurable impact across ESG priorities. As we move forward, we remain focused on creating enduring value for all our stakeholders while contributing to a more resilient, inclusive, and sustainable future.

10. Briefly describe the waste management practices adopted in your establishments. Describe the strategy adopted by your Company to reduce usage of hazardous and toxic chemicals in your products and processes and the practices adopted to manage such wastes.
Waste management is part of the Company’s Environment and EHS Management Systems across all locations. Each EPC site, manufacturing facility and campus follows a location-specific waste management plan based on waste type, quantity and disposal requirements. The focus is on the 3Rs - Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, to minimise waste generation and disposal.
Hazardous waste, where generated, is handled in line with Central or State Pollution Control Boards (CPCB/SPCB)
consents and applicable regulations, including the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Amendment Rules, 2025 and is disposed only through authorised agencies. Training and awareness are provided to employees and workers.
The Company’s product portfolio, comprising complex, engineered-to-order equipment for process industries and other sectors, does not involve hazardous or toxic chemicals, further reducing risk. Refer to the Natural Capital section of the Integrated Annual Report FY 2025–26 for details.
