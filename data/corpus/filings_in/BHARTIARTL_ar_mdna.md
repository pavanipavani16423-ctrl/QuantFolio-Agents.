About the Report
This Integrated Annual Report of Bharti Airtel Limited presents the Company’s financial and non-financial performance, strategic priorities and value creation approach. The report explains how Airtel utilises different resources to create value for stakeholders over time.
Reporting Period
This report covers the financial year from
April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026. Certain
sections include data from previous years
to provide context to stakeholders and
enable meaningful comparison of the
Company’s performance over time.
Reporting Boundary
The report covers information related
to the business operations of Bharti
Airtel Limited across its key operating
segments. Disclosures are presented
through the six capitals framework in
line with the International Integrated
Reporting approach.
All capitals, except Financial Capital,
present business information related
to Airtel’s operations in India, excluding
Indus Towers Limited, Airtel Payments
Bank Limited and Beetel Teletech Limited,
unless specified otherwise. Financial
Capital parameters are presented on a
standalone basis for Bharti Airtel Limited.
In certain sections of the report, historical
data may be restated where necessary
to reflect structural changes such as
consolidation impacts. These adjustments
are presented to ensure consistency
and comparability of information for
stakeholders.

This report follows the International
Integrated Reporting Framework.
Sustainability disclosures in the report
align with the Global Reporting Initiative
(GRI) Standards 2021, the United Nations
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
and the GSMA ESG Metrics for Mobile.
The report also aligns with the Business
Responsibility and Sustainability
Reporting (BRSR) requirements issued
by the Securities and Exchange Board of
India (SEBI).

Airtel: Building India’s Next
Billion Digital Moments
Dear Shareholders,
In FY2025-26, Airtel continued to chart a bold and
disciplined course, further strengthening its position as the second-largest global telecom operator by customer base.
Today, Brand Airtel is a flag bearer of India across 15 countries, built on the trust of our customers and our commitment
to serve them better every day with secure and reliable
connectivity solutions.
Over the last three decades, your Company has built world-
class digital infrastructure, fostered a culture of ownership and entrepreneurship and consistently placed the customer
at the heart of everything we do. These qualities at once
define Airtel and position your Company at the
forefront of digital transformation across markets.
Airtel’s investments are directed towards harnessing new
growth engines for India that rest on the foundation of a
resilient and secure digital infrastructure. These will be
shaped by the same principles that have guided Airtel’s
journey so far - a customer first approach, disciplined
execution and a long-term vision.
During the year, your Company executed the orderly
leadership transition with Gopal Vittal as Executive Vice
Chairman and Shashwat Sharma taking over as
MD and CEO of Airtel India.
Looking ahead, Airtel has the scale, capability and intent
to lead what comes next, powering India and Africa’s
growth through connectivity that is seamless and
innovation that is purposeful.
Macroeconomic Landscape
The past year unfolded against a backdrop of relentless
global flux intensifying geopolitical rivalries, the
reconfiguration of supply chains and a shifting trade
architecture that left few economies untouched. India,
however, is navigating these challenges, anchoring its policy in economic pragmatism and a heightened technology –
forward posture in its global engagements. Digitalisation
and digital infrastructure will continue to be prominent
supplements to India’s geo-economic strategy, in addition to the traditional markers of its growth.
India remains among the fastest-growing large economies,
underpinned by domestic demand, policy continuity and
expanding national capabilities. The digital economy is a
bedrock of this growth, reflected in the scale of adoption
across payments, public digital infrastructure, enterprise
transformation and Artificial Intelligence (AI)-led innovation.
AI has transformed systems of governance, commercial
offerings, workflows and communication in ways previously
unimaginable. The story of the impact of this tectonic
shift to the global landscape cannot be told without India.
India’s opportunity is unique in scale and pace. New Delhi
hosted the AI Impact Summit, outlining the significance of
responsible, ethical and accessible AI. It is my firm belief that this is India’s distinctive advantage. In just a decade, the country has showcased its digital prowess, lending the
power of connectivity to the last mile, the replete benefits of digitisation to its most vulnerable citizens, the emergence of a tech-native population at scale and perhaps most laudable, the foundation of truly frugal, accessible digital goods.
Through various partnerships, your Company is
actively working to ensure that the proliferation of AI is
broad-based, responsibility-driven and in line with India’s
technology priorities.
Policies supporting cloud, AI and digital infrastructure are expected to further accelerate investments and strengthen
the ecosystem. Building on this momentum, the long-term
tax holiday laid out by the Government of India for cloud
and AI-led data centre investments is a timely initiative,
expected to catalyse sustained capital inflows into digital
infrastructure, while assuring the people of India of
sovereign, secure data flows.
In this journey and indeed, at the heart of such
digital transformation, Airtel remains a reliable and
privileged partner to the nation. Your Company’s role
extends well beyond connectivity. We are building the
digital highways that enable economic activity, innovation
and inclusion at scale.
Over the last 10 years, your Company has invested
significantly with over H3.3 trillion in creating one of India’s strongest digital infrastructure platforms. The emergence
of the 5G economy is defining the next phase of growth,
enabling new use cases across industries. With widespread
network availability, 5G is accelerating the shift towards
high-speed, low-latency connectivity and unlocking new
avenues for innovation.
Further, we are leveraging advanced digital platforms to
simplify complexity, improve efficiency and enhance agility
across networks and operations. These capabilities are also
helping embed sustainability more deeply into the way we
build and manage our infrastructure.
Mobile
Your Company remained focused on its strategy: portfolio
premiumisation, winning with quality customers and
delivering them exceptional experience. This strategy has
yielded strong results, that are reflected in sustained revenue market share gains, industry-leading ARPU and robust
customer additions. During the year, we further expanded
our footprint into underserved geographies, reinforcing our
commitment to inclusive connectivity. Our rural acceleration programme continues to be a critical enabler of digital
proliferation and inclusion, with about 40,000 network sites rolled out over the last three years.

Plus network now carries half of all wireless data traffic on our network. The continued investment in network quality,
coverage and capacity expansion has been instrumental
in driving this adoption and remains a key differentiator
in the market.
Driving all these investments has been Airtel’s core
tenet of strengthening our future-ready, secure
network for all our customers.
Homes and Enterprise Business
The Home Broadband business saw another year of strong
progress. This segment is poised for accelerated growth,
underscored by continued benefits from structural trends.
Your Company is capitalising on the opportunity with
accelerated investments in fiber rollouts, fixed wireless
access (FWA) expansion along with sweating our expansive
distribution network. During the year, your Company added

Equally encouraging is the traction behind Airtel’s IPTV
offering, which is fast becoming a key pillar of your

and content into a single, seamless experience.

CPaaS, IoT and other Enterprise Solutions. FY2025-26 saw
further innovation through the launch of telco grade,
India’s sovereign cloud offering under Airtel Cloud. In a short span of time, your Company has secured over 24 deals,
extending its differentiated cloud offering to customers. Our data centre business delivered another year of consistent
performance and remains on track to expand its capacity
multi-fold in the years to come. Your Company is well
positioned to serve its customers with innovative and tailored digital solutions across Enterprises, Government and Small
and Medium Businesses.
