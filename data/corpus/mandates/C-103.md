# Investment Policy Statement - Kumar Family Office (client C-103) - Balanced Growth

Synthetic example document written for the QuantFolio-Agents project. Not a real client.

## Section 1. Purpose

1.1 This Investment Policy Statement (IPS) covers the US equity sleeve managed for the Kumar Family Office on behalf of three generations of the Kumar family.

## Section 2. Investment objectives

2.1 The objective is balanced growth: steady appreciation of capital with controlled drawdowns.

2.2 The family is willing to accept market risk but wants the portfolio to stay well diversified.

2.3 Risk aversion: the manager shall use a balanced weight on risk when choosing between portfolios (risk aversion coefficient 4 on the scale used by the firm's optimizer, where higher means more risk averse).

## Section 3. Portfolio construction

3.1 Number of holdings: the portfolio shall hold exactly 12 stocks.

3.2 Position limits: no single holding may exceed 12% of the portfolio, and every holding must be at least 4% of the portfolio.

3.3 Sector limits: no sector may exceed 30% of the portfolio.

3.4 Volatility limit: the expected annualised volatility of the portfolio must not exceed 22%.

## Section 4. Exclusions

4.1 Excluded sectors: none.

4.2 Excluded companies: PFE. A family member serves on the board of a direct competitor, so the family office does not hold Pfizer Inc. to avoid any conflict of interest.

## Section 5. Taxes

5.1 The family prefers to limit turnover in taxable accounts. Trades that only change a weight by less than 1 percentage point should be skipped.

## Section 6. Rebalancing and review

6.1 The portfolio is reviewed quarterly.

6.2 Every proposed rebalance must be approved by the family's chief investment officer before any order is placed.

## Section 7. Reporting

7.1 Reports must explain the expected return and risk of the new portfolio compared with the current one.
