# Investment Policy Statement - Sharma Family Trust (client C-201) - Growth with Values

Synthetic example document written for the QuantFolio-Agents project. Not a real client.

## Section 1. Purpose

1.1 This Investment Policy Statement (IPS) governs the listed Indian equity portfolio of the Sharma Family Trust, Pune, managed under a discretionary portfolio management arrangement.

1.2 The trust supports the education of the family's next generation and local scholarships, so the trustees want long-term growth from well-run Indian companies.

## Section 2. Investment objectives

2.1 The objective is long-term capital growth ahead of inflation over a horizon of at least seven years.

2.2 The trustees accept market ups and downs but want the portfolio spread across industries.

2.3 Risk aversion: the manager shall use a balanced weight on risk when choosing between portfolios (risk aversion coefficient 4 on the scale used by the firm's optimizer, where higher means more risk averse).

## Section 3. Portfolio construction

3.1 Number of holdings: the portfolio shall hold exactly 8 stocks.

3.2 Position limits: no single holding may exceed 18% of the portfolio, and every holding must be at least 5% of the portfolio.

3.3 Sector limits: no sector may exceed 35% of the portfolio.

3.4 Volatility limit: the expected annualised volatility of the portfolio must not exceed 22%.

3.5 Universe: only equity shares listed on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE), in Indian rupees. No derivatives, no borrowing.

## Section 4. Exclusions

4.1 Excluded sectors: none.

4.2 Excluded companies: ITC. The trustees do not invest in companies with a significant tobacco business.

## Section 5. Sustainability

5.1 The manager should review each holding's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) and flag material environmental or social risks to the trustees.

## Section 6. Rebalancing and review

6.1 The portfolio is reviewed every quarter.

6.2 Every proposed rebalance must be approved by the managing trustee before any order is placed.

## Section 7. Reporting

7.1 Reports must show each holding, its weight in rupees and percent, and the risks found in the companies' annual reports.
