# Investment Policy Statement - Meera Iyer (client C-101) - Conservative Income

Synthetic example document written for the QuantFolio-Agents project. Not a real client.

## Section 1. Purpose

1.1 This Investment Policy Statement (IPS) sets out the objectives, constraints and review process for the equity account of Meera Iyer, a retired schoolteacher aged 67. It guides the portfolio manager and any automated rebalancing system acting on her behalf.

1.2 The account funds part of her living expenses. Preserving capital matters more to her than maximising growth.

## Section 2. Investment objectives

2.1 The primary objective is capital preservation with modest growth, ahead of inflation over a full market cycle.

2.2 The client describes her risk tolerance as low. She would be uncomfortable with a loss of more than 15% in any single year.

2.3 Risk aversion: the manager shall weight risk heavily when choosing between portfolios (risk aversion coefficient 6 on the scale used by the firm's optimizer, where higher means more risk averse).

## Section 3. Portfolio construction

3.1 Number of holdings: the portfolio shall hold exactly 10 stocks.

3.2 Position limits: no single holding may exceed 15% of the portfolio, and every holding must be at least 4% of the portfolio.

3.3 Sector limits: no sector may exceed 30% of the portfolio.

3.4 Volatility limit: the expected annualised volatility of the portfolio must not exceed 18%.

## Section 4. Exclusions

4.1 Excluded sectors: none.

4.2 Excluded companies: none.

## Section 5. Liquidity and cash

5.1 The client keeps her emergency reserve in a separate savings account, so this account may be fully invested in equities.

## Section 6. Rebalancing and review

6.1 The portfolio is reviewed quarterly and rebalanced when any position drifts more than 5 percentage points from its target.

6.2 Every proposed rebalance must be approved by the client's advisor before any order is placed.

## Section 7. Reporting

7.1 The client receives a plain-language report after each rebalance explaining what changed and why.
