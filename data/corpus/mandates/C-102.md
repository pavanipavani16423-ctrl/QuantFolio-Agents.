# Investment Policy Statement - GreenLeaf Foundation (client C-102) - ESG Growth

Synthetic example document written for the QuantFolio-Agents project. Not a real client.

## Section 1. Purpose

1.1 This Investment Policy Statement (IPS) governs the equity endowment of the GreenLeaf Foundation, a charitable foundation that funds reforestation and clean-water projects.

1.2 The Board of Trustees requires the endowment to reflect the Foundation's environmental mission.

## Section 2. Investment objectives

2.1 The objective is long-term growth of the endowment to support grant-making over a horizon of more than ten years.

2.2 The Foundation accepts above-average short-term volatility in exchange for higher expected long-term returns.

2.3 Risk aversion: the manager shall use a moderate weight on risk when choosing between portfolios (risk aversion coefficient 3 on the scale used by the firm's optimizer, where higher means more risk averse).

## Section 3. Portfolio construction

3.1 Number of holdings: the portfolio shall hold exactly 8 stocks.

3.2 Position limits: no single holding may exceed 20% of the portfolio, and every holding must be at least 5% of the portfolio.

3.3 Sector limits: no sector may exceed 40% of the portfolio.

3.4 Volatility limit: the expected annualised volatility of the portfolio must not exceed 25%.

## Section 4. Exclusions

4.1 Excluded sectors: Energy. The Foundation does not invest in companies whose primary business is the exploration, production or refining of oil and gas.

4.2 Excluded companies: none beyond the sector exclusion.

## Section 5. ESG engagement

5.1 The manager should flag holdings with material climate-related or environmental risks disclosed in their annual reports, so the Trustees can engage with those companies.

## Section 6. Rebalancing and review

6.1 The portfolio is reviewed semi-annually by the Investment Committee.

6.2 Every proposed rebalance must be approved by the Investment Committee chair before any order is placed.

## Section 7. Reporting

7.1 Reports must list each holding, its weight, and any environmental risks found in its filings.
